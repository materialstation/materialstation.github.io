Focusing on a single problem can create so much internal stress. Especially when we have a deadline, life turns into a nightmare. We almost forget the meaning of life and, of course, of time.

**What if forgetting the meaning of time is a sign of good things?**

It is well known that scientists, entrepreneurs and artists devote themselves to their work while chasing new discoveries. They keep repeating things until they are sure of the final result.

**How did they reach their goals in a limited time?**

When you fully focus on the moment, without recalling the negative memories of the past or making wrong assumptions about the future, you have a chance to become part of a divine source of reality.

Maya Angelou emphasizes the importance of this in her book **[“I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Know_Why_the_Caged_Bird_Sings "Source: en.wikipedia.org")**. When I finished reading it, I realized that I had highlighted so many sentences. Even her heartbreaking childhood was not an excuse for her to stop fighting for her dreams. Growing up among abusive people made her more determined to find her own path. Because of the trauma she experienced at the age of eight, she stopped speaking for almost five years; however, she was clever enough to ***[turn her wounds into wisdom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNMmDN_m_9U&t=52s "Source: youtube.com")***.

In the following years, diving into the sea of literature and quietly observing people helped her create the space she needed for self-development. While memorizing long Shakespeare poems, which she loved to do, she lost track of time.

After spending almost 3 hours in front of an optical microscope, hoping to find the best material to make a device, I was able to see the big picture. A tiny dust particle yelled at me the truth I had been hiding from.

We may not have the same experiences in life, yet we so bravely judge each other based on our perceptions. The best way to get rid of this dilemma is to keep doing what we love. Then success will be our next station, despite people’s discouraging comments.

That is exactly what she meant when she said: **[“Pursue the things you love doing, and then do them so well that people can’t take their eyes off you.”](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/290210-instead-pursue-the-things-you-love-doing-and-then-do "Source: goodreads.com")**

The heart-shaped dust particle changed my perception of time, and at the end of the day, I put what I experienced into words:

*Time does not exist for those whose love comes from the heart.*
